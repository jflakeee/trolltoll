## 1. Overview

Combat automatically attacks nearby enemies when the player's cooldown permits; movement is direct via keyboard/touch, with optional auto-navigation. Defeating an enemy immediately grants gold, XP and a permanent stat increase without a loot pickup. Bosses have windup attacks, unlock progression and first-clear relic bonuses.

## 2. Detailed Design

- The game initializes 21 ordinary enemies (slime, goblin, golem variants across three distance bands) and three bosses. Enemies have HP, attack, XP, a stat reward category, cooldown and death/respawn timers.
- `update(dt)` handles input, auto-navigation, zone clamps, regeneration, enemy AI, damage, death, target selection and automatic attacks.
- Combat candidate selection includes living enemies within 115 world units; an explicitly selected candidate within range overrides mode-based prioritization. Modes are `auto`, `combat` and `gather`. Nearby resource nodes can compete for the same automatic action loop.
- Player attacks when `p.cd<=0` and target distance is below 115, dealing `totalAttack()` directly to enemy HP; cooldown resets to 0.55 seconds. `gain(e)` runs when health falls to zero.
- Ordinary enemies respawn after 4 seconds, bosses after 18 seconds; boss first-clear flags persist. Ordinary enemies may respawn at a position relative to the player, clamped to the unlocked zone.
- `gain(e)` grants +3 gold and enemy XP immediately, processes repeated level-up thresholds, adds +1 attack / +1 defense / +2 max HP based on enemy `stat`; the HP category also restores +2 HP.
- First boss clear grants +1 relic, +5 base attack, +3 defense, +20 max HP and +20 current HP; subsequent kills do not repeat that bonus.
- Player death restores HP and returns to origin without erasing accumulated growth. Enemy attacks and boss windups are handled in `update(dt)`.

## 4. Edge Cases and Dependencies

**Handled:** attack cooldown prevents repeated attacks each frame; dead enemies are excluded from targeting; enemy HP depletion triggers one kill reward before respawn; first boss clear is tracked by ID; player death preserves growth; zone gates restrict movement.

**Risks:** targeting preference can be overridden by a selected target; auto-navigation uses different interaction reach values than combat candidate selection; combat and gathering are in one frame loop, making manual/automatic priority a UX risk. Boss windup timing and hit zones require separate mobile playtest. **INTENT UNKNOWN:** Is guaranteed permanent growth from every kill intended indefinitely, and should player death have no currency penalty?
